# Phase 1 — P2300R10 cross-paper validation

**Status:** Phase 1 complete (210 sentences labeled, 0..209). Phases 2+
(remaining 2,587 sentences, mostly wording / spec text) deferred.
**Scoring:** nli-small only (zeroshot-large run was still pending at
writeup time; will update when it completes).

## Sample under test

P2300R10 is the `std::execution` paper: 6,138-line markdown, 17 chunks,
2,797 decomposed sentences. Phase 1 covers the first 210 sentences:
abstract, motivation, design goals, end-user examples (basic, scan,
async I/O), Sudoku/file-copy/networking example narratives, algorithm
and scheduler implementation walkthroughs.

Gold distribution: **TARGET=93, CONTEXT=111, SKIP=6** (44/53/3 %).
Different mix from P4003R3 (47/37/16 %) — much more tutorial CONTEXT,
almost no SKIP fragments in this section.

## Baseline accuracy (production hypothesis labels)

| classifier | margin | acc | T→S | T→C | TARGET recall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| nli-small | (0.05, 0.4) | 0.462 | 3 | 50 | 40/93 (43%) |
| nli-small | (0.05, 0.6) | 0.486 | 1 | 52 | 40/93 (43%) |
| nli-small | (0.0, 0.4)  | 0.443 | 3 | 34 | 40/93 (43%) |

**43% TARGET recall** is a strong negative signal. More than half the
claims the paper makes — including the central motivation
paragraphs (sids 5/6/7/8) and many implementation-definition
sentences (sid 68 `1. \`async_read\` is a pipeable sender adaptor.`,
sid 75 `3. \`dynamic_buffer\` is an aggregate struct...`) — are being
silently demoted to CONTEXT.

The thesis is caught (sid=0, target=0.396) but barely.

## Diagnosis

Phase 1 reveals that **list-marker prefixes are NOT the cause** of
T→C misses, despite our prior hypothesis. We re-scored all 21 list-
prefixed missed-TARGET sentences with the leading `1.` / `- ` / `* `
stripped:

> 1 / 21 sentences flipped to TARGET after stripping the marker.

The remaining 20 stayed CONTEXT. The hypothesis label `"A statement
of fact or opinion."` simply does not entail implementation-style
prose like `"Defines a sender that aggregates another sender and the
invocable..."` or `"Customizes execution::schedule to return an
instance of the sender type \`_sender\`."`. These read as neither
"fact" nor "opinion" to an NLI model; they're descriptions of
specifications and implementations.

## Alternative TARGET hypotheses

We tested four broader phrasings against the gold (`SKIP_HYPO`
unchanged):

| TARGET hypothesis | P2300R10 acc | recall | T→S | P4003R3 acc | recall | T→S |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| `"A statement of fact or opinion."` (baseline) | 0.486 | 43% | 1 | 0.467 | 62% | 0 |
| `"A claim, proposal, or assertion."` | 0.433 | 39% | 0 | — | — | — |
| `"A statement about software behavior, design, or specification."` | **0.533** | 81% | 0 | **0.504** | 78% | 1 |
| `"A statement describing what something does, is, or proposes."` | 0.443 | **98%** | 0 | 0.482 | **97%** | 0 |
| `"An assertion of fact, design choice, or behavior."` | 0.462 | 88% | 0 | 0.460 | 91% | 0 |

Both candidate hypotheses **cross-validate** — they don't tune to
P2300R10 at the expense of P4003R3.

## What this changes about the design

**The TARGET hypothesis label is the dominant tuning knob, not
ensemble shape or margin.** Switching from
`"A statement of fact or opinion."` to
`"A statement describing what something does, is, or proposes."`:

- P2300R10 Phase 1 TARGET recall: 43% → **98%** (+55 pts)
- P4003R3 full corpus TARGET recall: 62% → **97%** (+35 pts)
- T→S miss-fires on both papers: 0

These gains exceed *anything* the prior ensemble study produced. The
new hypothesis works with nli-small alone, no extra compute, no
second model load. Concretely it now **TARGETs essentially every
claim-bearing sentence in both papers**, the property the original
cascade design tried to recover via an oracle pass.

Cost: accuracy doesn't strictly improve. The broader hypothesis
over-tags some CONTEXT-style sentences as TARGET, lowering F1_CONTEXT.
For Step 1's job — preventing irrecoverable SKIP of claim-bearing
sentences, then handing tags to a downstream LLM that consumes the
whole chunk anyway — this is the correct trade. **CONTEXT-misclassified
as TARGET is harmless. The downstream LLM still sees the sentence;
the tag is just a hint.**

## Failure modes still present

Two TARGETs miss on P4003R3 and two on P2300R10 even with the new
hypothesis. Inspecting them, they're all sentences that are
genuinely on the TARGET/CONTEXT boundary — short rhetorical
restatements that arguably could be labeled either way (e.g.
"That is its entire job." after "The executor queues..."). I'm not
sure they're worth chasing further; the gold itself was conservative
toward TARGET on those.

The single T→S miss-fire on P4003R3 with the new hypothesis is
sid=106-equivalent — a comparative claim with a markdown link
("In contrast, the TBB example ... [leaks memory](link)") that the
SKIP hypothesis catches as "page metadata". This argues for a
follow-up: strip or replace markdown link URLs at preprocessing
time, since they're noise to a sentence classifier.

## What this means for the cascade

Recommendation downgrade: **the cascade is no longer the primary
recommendation.** With the improved TARGET hypothesis on nli-small
alone we already hit 97-98% TARGET recall and 0 T→S misses on both
test papers. The cascade adds runtime cost (loading zeroshot-large,
running it on ~70% of sentences) for incremental gains on the
remaining 2-3% TARGET recall.

We'll know more once the pending zeroshot-large run on P2300R10
finishes (~1 hour from writeup time). If zeroshot-large catches
those last few TARGETs *and* the cascade fast-path keeps the
common case cheap, the cascade may still be worth it. If
zeroshot-large doesn't meaningfully add recall on top of the
new hypothesis, **ship the hypothesis change alone** and skip
the cascade entirely.

## Open questions / what to label next

1. **Phase 2** (sids 210-1500): the formal specification sections of
   P2300R10, which contain dense wording-style sentences. These
   should be SKIPped en masse (they're spec text, not claims) but
   the current `_blank_non_prose` doesn't catch them. Phase 2
   labeling will quantify the cost.
2. **Multi-link sentence preprocessing**: strip or replace
   `[text](url)` constructs with `text` before classification, to
   eliminate the single remaining T→S misfire pattern.
3. **Cascade ablation with new hypothesis**: re-run the cascade
   shapes from `findings.md` using the new TARGET hypothesis, on
   Phase 1, when zeroshot-large scores arrive.
